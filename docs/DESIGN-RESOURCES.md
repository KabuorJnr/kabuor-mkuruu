# Design resources

Tools to keep polishing the Mkurugenzi rack: motion, visuals, icons and imagery.

| Tool | What it is | Where it helps this project |
|---|---|---|
| [MOVO](https://movo.video) | Browser-based animation tool with AI motion templates; no downloads or installs | Short promo clips of the rack and new drops for Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp Status |
| [Surface](https://surface-motion.com) | Surface Component Studio, for designing animated UI components | Prototyping micro-interactions: the garment turn on hover, bag button bump, checkout steps |
| [Sketch Tools](https://tools.sketchdesign.club) | Browser platform for motion and audio-reactive visuals | Hero backgrounds and launch visuals for drop announcements |
| [Dither Icons](https://dithered.dev) | 89 original animated SVG icons for React (dither, solid and outline styles), MIT licensed | Bag, hanger, delivery and payment icons in the bag and checkout panel. MIT licence: keep the notice when bundling |
| [OnePrompt](https://onepromptdesign.com) | Curated AI image prompts and visual references for cinematic visuals | Prompt ideas for future garment renders and campaign imagery (see `tools/pack_atlas.py` for the render pipeline) |

## Before using anything in production

- Check each tool's licence and terms for commercial use, and keep any required attribution.
- Generated or AI-assisted imagery of products should be approved by the brand before it goes on the live store.
- Keep the rack's rules: no third-party scripts, fonts or tracking loaded at runtime unless they are self-hosted and audited.
